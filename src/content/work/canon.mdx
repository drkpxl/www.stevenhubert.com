I executive produced this one at Mondo Robot. The brief was a real-time video compositing app for Canon: a child writes, draws, or picks images for their holiday wish, and a near-real-time render gives them back a sharable video of Santa receiving the wish on a Canon PIXMA printer at the North Pole.

![North Pole Pixma](/images/North-Pole-PIXMA-Image-Snow-Globe-Event.jpg)
*Canon North Pole Pixma Start Page*

The Facebook experience was fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile, with custom creation tools to let kids draw, type, or assemble their wish from a set of preselected images. Most of the magic was speed. The wish had to render into a finished video fast enough that the user felt the moment of seeing it land in front of Santa.

![Customize Screen](/images/PismaWishList.png)
*Customize Screen*

<Video poster="/images/canon1.jpg" src="/videos/canon.mp4" />

Under the hood, a fleet of AWS render servers ran custom C++ and FFMPEG code to composite the wish onto the video with believable shadow and highlight, then encode the result on demand for both desktop and mobile playback. It was a novel piece of infrastructure for the moment, and it held up under real holiday-season traffic.
