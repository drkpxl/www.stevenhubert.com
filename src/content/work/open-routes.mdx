Open Routes is a free, open-source platform for uploading and sharing GPX or FIT files for hiking, mountain biking, gravel, road cycling, trail running, and other ground sports. I built it because the existing options are either paywalled, locked to a single sport's community, or fenced behind an app store.

![A specific Open Routes trail page](/images/open-routes/2026-05-Maryland%20Mountain%20Fast%20Money%20Quick%20Loop%20-%20OpenRoutes.png)
*An individual route page (Maryland Mountain Fast Money Quick Loop) with elevation, distance, and the GPX rendered on a topo map*

It runs on MapLibre with OpenFreeMap tiles and OpenStreetMap data. Routes can be filtered by sport, and mountain-bike entries pick up IMBA trail ratings where the source data has them. The stack is intentionally boring so the costs of running it stay near zero.

![Open Routes upload flow](/images/open-routes/2026-05-Upload%20Route%20-%20OpenRoutes.png)
*The contributor upload flow: drop in a GPX or FIT file, tag the sport, and it lands on the public map*

Open-source matters here. The whole point of the project is the long-tail trails that local riders know about but never make it into the proprietary databases. The only way that data stays accessible is if the platform holding it does not eventually want it walled off behind a subscription.
