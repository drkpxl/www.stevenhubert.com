Jabber is a small web app that ships a single question to a screen and gets out of the way. No accounts, no scores, no telemetry, no return-visit prompt. The product brief was intentionally short, and the discipline of the build was deciding what to leave out.

![A Jabber question card](https://placehold.co/1200x675?text=Jabber+question+card)
*A typical Jabber question: "What's the most peaceful place you can think of?"*

The question set is curated rather than generated, deep-linkable so a good one can be shared by URL, and tuned for families with kids between four and twelve. The original problem was my own dinner table. The product worked, so I shipped it.

I keep this entry in the portfolio because it is a working counter-example to a pattern I see often: products that quietly accumulate analytics, accounts, and engagement loops because nobody decided not to. Sometimes the right answer is to ship something that respects its audience and walks away. The judgment to make that call holds at any scale.
