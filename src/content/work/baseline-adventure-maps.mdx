Baseline Adventure Maps is the physical-product half of the workflow that Laser Map Studio runs at scale. Customers upload a GPX or pick from a stocked route, and a wooden, layered, topographic map is produced and shipped from a single studio in Denver.

![A finished custom adventure map](https://placehold.co/1200x675?text=Layered+wooden+adventure+map)
*A custom trail map: layered wood, topographic contours, finished and framed before shipping*

There are four product lines: Cruise Maps for inland routes, Park Maps for the larger 11 by 11 inch national-park editions, Iconic Trails for the routes that get requested most, and Custom Trail Maps for any GPX a customer uploads. Every piece is layered for depth and arrives framed and ready to hang.

The cadence is deliberate. Production runs one order at a time rather than in batches, which is the only way to keep the finish quality where the price expects it to be. The product is a keepsake, not a print-on-demand catalog item, and it sits in the same category as a piece of art commissioned from a craftsperson rather than a poster from a print shop.

Pairing this with Laser Map Studio gave me both ends of the workflow: the software people pay to use, and the physical product the same data can become when the cutting happens here.
