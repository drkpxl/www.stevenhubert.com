Baseline Adventure Maps is the made-to-order side of the same craft Laser Map Studio runs at scale. Customers upload a GPX of a trail they love, or pick one of the iconic routes I stock, and I cut, layer, and frame a wooden map by hand in Denver.

![A finished custom adventure map](https://placehold.co/1200x675?text=Layered+wooden+adventure+map)
*A custom trail map: layered wood, topographic contours, hand-finished in Denver*

There are four product lines: Cruise Maps for inland routes, Park Maps for the larger 11 by 11 inch national-park editions, Iconic Trails for the routes that get requested most, and Custom Trail Maps for anything someone uploads as a GPX. Every piece is hand-finished, layered for depth, and arrives framed and ready to hang.

The pitch I made on the site is the truest one I could make: I am a working dad in Denver, and I make Baseline Adventure Maps one order at a time. That cadence matters. The product is a keepsake and not a print-on-demand catalog item, and it lives in the same category as a piece of art commissioned from a craftsperson, not a poster from a print shop.

Pairing this with Laser Map Studio gave me both ends of the workflow: the software people pay to use, and the physical objects the same data can become when somebody else does the cutting.
