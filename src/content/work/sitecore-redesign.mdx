Alterra owns and operates 15 ski resorts. Each one has its own audience, its own seasonality, and its own marketing team running its campaigns. I led the design and product strategy for a homepage system that let those teams express each resort's character while sharing one codebase, one CMS, and one engineering pipeline.

Background video does most of the heavy lifting on these homepages. It gives a newly arriving guest a sense of place, mountain, mood, terrain, before they even start reading.

![Deer Valley 2022](/images/DeerValleyHomepage2022.png)
*Deer Valley Homepage*

Deer Valley uses the pattern well. Beautiful landscape under a persistent booking widget so the path to transact is always one tap away.

Each of the 15 resorts plays on the same patterns slightly differently and offers different mountain experiences, so the framework had to flex without cracking.

![All Resort Homepages](/images/AllResortList.jpg)
*All Sites Homepages*

I designed the backend experience with as much care as the front. Mountain marketing teams without a technical background need to update layout and content as conditions, campaigns, and openings shift through the season. The admin was built to let them do that confidently.

![Deer Valley Sitecore Admin](/images/DVHomeAdmin.png)
*Homepage Booking Widget Customization Admin Experience*

The shared backbone means the engineering team can ship a new feature once and have it available everywhere, rather than building it 15 times for 15 sites. Each resort gets the look and the rhythm it wants, the codebase stays single, and the team's velocity scales with the portfolio instead of fighting it.
