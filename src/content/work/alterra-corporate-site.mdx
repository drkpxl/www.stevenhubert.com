Alterramtn.co is the corporate face of Alterra Mountain Company. It is the page institutional investors, prospective hires, journalists, and partner brands land on. The audience is not the same audience as ikonpass.com or any single resort site, and the page has to do a different kind of work.

![Alterra corporate homepage](https://placehold.co/1200x675?text=alterramtn.co+homepage)
*The corporate site introduces the operating company rather than selling a pass*

I led the strategy, content direction, and visual direction for the site. The bar was to present an operating company with credibility. Nineteen owned destinations, 60+ available through the Ikon Pass, plus a portfolio of adjacent businesses (Ski Butlers, Aspenware, Alpine Aerotech) that most pass-holders do not know exist. None of that is the right tone for a consumer site, all of it is the right tone here.

The harder editorial decision was around the workforce and community pillars. Employee housing, foundation work, environmental commitments: those pieces are not marketing for skiers, they are operating-company concerns that matter to investors and prospective senior hires. Getting them onto the page without making it read like a CSR brochure took more iteration than the visual design did.
