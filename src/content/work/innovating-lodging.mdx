Before this project, the Winter Park lodging page was effectively a brochure. Guests could browse what was offered, but to actually transact they had to leave for Inntopia's site. I led the UX research and product work to integrate Inntopia's APIs directly into the Winter Park experience, so guests could see live inventory, real pricing, packages, and richer room information without leaving the resort's domain.

![Winter Park 2016](/images/WinterPark2016.jpg)
*Before Redesign*

![Winter Park 2022](/images/WinterPark2022.jpg)
*After Redesign*

The before and after above tell most of the story. The redesigned view loads the page with the information a guest actually needs to commit, and keeps them on Winter Park-owned pages all the way through checkout.

![Winter Park Cart 2022](/images/WinterParkCart2022.jpg)
*Cart Redesign, damage waiver upsell*

Inside the cart we layered a lightweight AI-driven upsell for related products, such as a damage waiver, sized to feel like a useful nudge rather than an interruption. Most of a lodging decision happens long before someone hits add-to-cart, so the cart's job was to clean up that final step without making it feel transactional.
