[WeChooseTheMoon.org](http://www.wechoosethemoon.org) is an interactive re-creation of the Apollo 11 mission, inspired by JFK's grand vision. More than 3.5 million people have taken the journey, a million of them on the first day to watch the launch in real time the way the world did in 1969.

![Homepage](/images/moon1.jpg)
*We Choose The Moon Homepage*

I produced the project at Domani Studios in partnership with the Martin Agency and the JFK Library. There was no real playbook for what we were building. The site still holds up today, provided you can still run Adobe Flash.

The hardest production problem was scale. We needed to support a million concurrent users watching the mission play out in real time. We solved it with 200 servers from AOL, Akamai for video streaming, and Limelight for the four full days of original mission audio, all synced to play back in lockstep. It worked. CNN [featured the launch](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K_iQfqqILUQ), and the project held the #1 trending spot on Twitter for about six hours that day.

The work went on to win five Cannes Gold Lions, an unprecedented haul for digital at the time, along with FWA Site of the Year, SXSW for Motion Graphics, and a London International Gold. [Adobe produced a featurette](http://tv.adobe.com/en/watch/traditional-media-in-transition/domani-studios-we-choose-the-moon/) on the project as well.
