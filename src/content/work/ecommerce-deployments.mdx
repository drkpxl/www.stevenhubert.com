Alterra operates three separate ecommerce systems. AMP is the custom-built platform that sells Ikon Pass and Local Pass products. Inntopia handles lodging and packages. Aspenware runs lessons, rentals, and single-day lift tickets. These systems were chosen at different times by different teams to solve different problems, and they were never meant to feel like one experience.

![Where the three systems hand off](https://placehold.co/1200x675?text=AMP+%2F+Inntopia+%2F+Aspenware+handoffs)
*Where the three platforms touch the guest journey, and where my team owns the connective tissue*

My responsibility is not the platforms themselves. It is the handoffs, the consistent design language around them, and the moments where a passholder has to move from one to another without the journey shattering. That sounds smaller than it is. The passholder buying lodging on Inntopia and a lesson on Aspenware does not care which vendor runs which checkout. They notice when those moments do not feel coherent.
