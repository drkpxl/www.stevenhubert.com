Ski Colorado was a multi-mountain pass product anchored on the Rocky Mountain Super Pass+, and the site selling it was overdue for a rebuild. The old site tried to sell the full pass portfolio at once. The analytics were clear that the RSMP+ was the hero and deserved hero treatment.

We rebuilt around that single product. Sections were dedicated and easy to scan, the FAQ answered the questions Guest Services at Winter Park reported hearing most, and the early signals from heat mapping and NPS came back encouraging.

On the platform side we went static. [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) for build, [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/) for hosting, the same stack we'd been using on the more dynamic [CMH](https://www.canadianmountainholidays.com/) site. For the marketing team we wired in [Forestry](https://forestry.io/) as a lightweight CMS, and layered in [Convert](https://www.convert.com/) for A/B testing and personalization, so Texas customers see different messaging than Colorado or New York customers.
