I executive produced A Gentleman's Call at Grey NY for Ketel One. The brief was straightforward: ask American men to describe how they would make the world a better place if handed $100K, then turn the strongest entries into something worth talking about.

![Ketel One Homepage](/images/ketel1.jpg)
*Ketel One Contest Homepage*

We built the entry tool inside a custom Facebook application, narrowed the pool to five finalists, and commissioned Vice to shoot a profile video for each. The finalist videos lived on social, the public voted, and a winner took the prize.

![Video Screen](/images/ketel2.jpg)

Entrants could customize the visual direction of their pitch and push their network to vote, with a custom CMS behind the experience for real-time moderation.

**Winning Video**

The winner was Corbin Clay, owner of [Azure Furniture](https://azurefurniture.com/), who makes beetle-kill furniture. His pieces are so good that I bought a custom kitchen table from him a few months after he won.

<Video poster="/images/k2.jpg" src="/videos/ToasttoCorbinClay.mp4" />

Tens of thousands of substantive entries came in. Picking a contest format that asked something real of entrants, rather than a standard sweepstakes, generated meaningful earned media coverage (Wall Street Journal, Interview Magazine, Business Insider, Dezeen, and others) that did the heavy lifting our budget could not.
