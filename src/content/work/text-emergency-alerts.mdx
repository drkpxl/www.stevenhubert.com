This product came out of a conversation with a Winter Park ski patroller named Will. Ski patrol communication runs over phone or CB radio: the patroller on scene calls a supervisor, who calls A, B, and C depending on the situation. Will's idea was to expand the QTA platform with private messaging groups that send one-way alerts at each stage of escalation, so an injury, a mechanical issue, and a personnel issue each route along their own path.

The use case is urgent, so the UI does as little as possible. Three sections: add recipients, create or edit groups, send a message. The product is not novel; SaaS tools in this space exist, but they are not shaped around a ski resort's operational reality or its budget. A modest Heroku bill plus the per-message cost runs this at a real resort, and it rolled out across the Intrawest family.

The natural next step is two-way communication; an inbox the right people can monitor. That needs a clear 24/7 ownership story before it ships, which is why it hasn't yet.
