Alterra owns and operates 15 ski resorts. Each one has its own audience, its own seasonality, and its own marketing team running its campaigns. I led the design and product strategy for a homepage system that let those teams express each resort's character while sharing one codebase, one CMS, and one engineering pipeline.

Background video does most of the heavy lifting on the homepages. It gives a newly arriving guest a sense of place, mountain, mood, terrain, before they start reading. Each of the 15 resorts plays on the same patterns slightly differently, so the framework had to flex without cracking.

I designed the backend experience with as much care as the front. Mountain marketing teams without a technical background need to update layout and content as conditions, campaigns, and openings shift through the season. The admin lets them do that confidently.

The shared backbone means engineering ships a new feature once and has it available everywhere, instead of building it 15 times. Each resort gets the look and the rhythm it wants, the codebase stays single, and the team's velocity scales with the portfolio.
