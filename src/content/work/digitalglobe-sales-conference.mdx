I executive produced the digital and print package for DigitalGlobe's annual sales conference at Mondo Robot. The keynote intro is the moment everyone in the room is waiting for, and the year's theme was Go Big. The VP of Sales jumped out of a plane on camera right before walking on stage.

The intro video played on three 40-foot screens to about a thousand people.

<Video poster="/images/dg1.jpg" src="/videos/DGIntroFull.mp4" />

He walked into the room still in skydiving gear, ready to start. It set exactly the tone the year called for.

The rest of the keynote ran on custom 3D animations cued live to the speakers' pacing and beats. The team worked carefully so the visuals supported the speaker rather than competing with them. Posters, gobos, and printed handouts carried the same visual language across the venue so the experience held together from the moment people walked in.
