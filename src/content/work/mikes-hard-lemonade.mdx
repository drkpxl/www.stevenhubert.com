I executive produced a year of social campaigns for Mike's Hard at Grey NY. The brief was to give Mike's a real voice in the social space on a budget that was a fraction of what the bigger beer brands were spending. A dozen or so swings followed; these are the ones I still think about.

### Mike's Mariachi

For Cuatro de Mayo we commissioned a local Brooklyn band, dressed them as Mariachis, and had them play songs written in real time by a team of copywriters working from live tweets.

![Mike's Mariachi](/images/Mariachi+Live+Stream.jpg)
*Mike's Mariachi Live Stream*

The interesting production problem was getting lyrics written, legally cleared, and onto the teleprompter fast enough to keep the live stream live. The [workflow](/images/workflow.png) we built ran like this:

1. Tweets land in a Google doc
2. A copywriter claims a tweet
3. Lyrics get written
4. Creative Director approves
5. Legal approves
6. Lyrics load into the teleprompter for the band

Live show ran clean. The campaign was a breakout for the brand and pulled coverage from [AgencySpy](http://www.mediabistro.com/agencyspy/mikes-hard-lemonade-band-will-mariachi-ize-just-about-any-song_b32710), [Ad Week](http://www.adweek.com/adfreak/mikes-hard-lemonade-welcomes-some-unexpected-visitors-142143), and a few others.
