The Ikon Pass app is the mobile companion to the pass. It is where passholders check the conditions, find a mountain, hit the slopes with their pass loaded for scan, and increasingly tap to pay on the mountain.

![Ikon Pass app home screen](https://placehold.co/1200x675?text=App+home+screen)
*The app's home view: conditions, partner mountains, and pass scan*

For the 26/27 season the app picked up a meaningfully improved map experience, seamless on-mountain payment, an updated profile, and on-the-go notifications for the moments that matter. My team led the design and product direction for those releases.
