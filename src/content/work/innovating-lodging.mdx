Before this project, the Winter Park lodging page was a brochure. Guests could browse what was offered but had to leave for Inntopia's site to actually transact. I led the UX research and product work to integrate Inntopia's APIs directly into the Winter Park experience, so guests could see live inventory, real pricing, packages, and richer room information without leaving the resort's domain.

![Winter Park 2022](/images/WinterPark2022.jpg)
*Lodging detail with live inventory and pricing*

The redesigned page loads the information a guest actually needs to commit and keeps them on Winter Park pages all the way through checkout. In the cart we layered a lightweight AI-driven upsell for related products, sized to feel like a useful nudge rather than an interruption. Most of a lodging decision happens long before someone hits add-to-cart, so the cart's job was to clean up the final step without making it feel transactional.
