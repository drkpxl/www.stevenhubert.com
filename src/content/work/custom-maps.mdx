Custom Maps is the in-house program I established to commission high-touch, artist-led 3D printed maps of Alterra's resorts. These are not the kind of laser-cut wooden trail maps you can buy at the lodge gift shop. They are large-format, sculptural, individually designed pieces that anchor lobbies, executive offices, and high-end suite rooms.

![A custom 3D printed resort map](https://placehold.co/1200x675?text=3D+printed+resort+map)
*One of the resort pieces. Each is individually designed and finished before installation*

The brief on every piece is the same. The geometry has to be accurate to the actual terrain. The finish has to feel like an art object and not a model. The piece has to read at a glance for a guest who has never been on the mountain and reward a closer look from a guest who has skied there for twenty years.
